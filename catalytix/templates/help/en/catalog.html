The Catalogue lists every book you are allowed to see. The search box at the top searches title, subtitle, author, publisher, tags, category, shelf mark, location and ISBN – you can also type an ISBN or scan it in with a barcode scanner.

Matching books appear as you type (from two characters), with author, shelf mark and free copies. A click – or the arrow keys and Enter – opens the book straight away; Enter without a selection shows all matches in the catalogue as before.

  • Filter by group, location and status (Available / Borrowed).
  • Sort by title, author, recently added or year of publication.
  • View: on the right, switch between Tiles with covers and a compact List. Your browser remembers the choice.
  • Click a cover or title to open the book page with its location, shelf mark and number of available copies.
