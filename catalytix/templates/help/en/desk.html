The Circulation desk is built for the counter: the input field stays active, so you just scan one book after another.

Lending (Loan tab):

  1. User: type a name, username or email – matching people appear in a list; pick one with the arrow keys and Enter or by clicking. On the right you see what the person already has; Change starts with the next person.{% if f.ldap_on and f.ldap_service_account %} The list also contains people from Active Directory who have never signed in (new from AD) – from three typed characters on. Known people appear immediately; matches from AD are added a moment later. Their account is only created with the first loan – with name, email and groups from AD. Afterwards they sign in with their AD password.{% endif %}
  2. Book: scan or type an ISBN, shelf mark or title and click Lend. The camera icon scans with the camera. A shelf mark matches regardless of upper or lower case – even one made only of digits. To use the internal book number (shown in the address of the book page, e.g. …/buecher/12), type it with a leading #, i.e. #12.

With Lend immediately on an exact match (ISBN, shelf mark) (on by default) the book is lent without another click when the ISBN, shelf mark or #number matches exactly one book – the field is ready for the next one straight away. Search text such as a title or author never lends automatically; a list of results with its own Lend buttons appears instead.

The app automatically checks that the account is active, the person may borrow the book (group), a copy is available, the book is not reference only and they don't already have it{% if f.max_loans %}, and whether the limit of {{ f.max_loans }} loans has been reached{% endif %}. The book is due after {{ f.loan_days }} days.{% if f.mail_on and f.loan_mail %} The person gets a confirmation by email.{% endif %}

Searching the collection: type a title, author, shelf mark, ISBN or tag into the book field and matching books from your collection appear – with location, available copies and how many are on loan. Books the selected person may not borrow (other group) are listed last and marked. Picking one with the arrow keys and Enter or by clicking lends it (with Lend immediately if there is exactly one match) or shows it. Pressing Enter without picking a suggestion searches for the typed text and shows a list of hits – only an exact match (shelf mark or ISBN) is preselected. Books the person already has are also listed last. Without a selected person the search only shows the collection. Reference-only books (groups without lending) are never offered for lending, but are found for returns. On the Return tab, books on loan come first. A barcode scanner works as before.

Returns (Return tab): scan the book. With Return immediately on an exact match (ISBN, shelf mark) the loan is closed straight away when the ISBN or shelf mark matches exactly one open loan; otherwise pick the right one and click Check in.{% if f.reservations %} If the book is reserved, the message says “Please put it aside – reserved for …”: keep it aside for that person, who is notified. When they come to the desk and you pick them, the book appears at the top under Set aside for … and can be lent with one click. A book's queue is shown on its page, where you can also delete reservations.{% endif %}
