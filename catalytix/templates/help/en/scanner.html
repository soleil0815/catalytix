USB or Bluetooth barcode scanners work without any setup: they act like a keyboard. Click into the input field and scan – the scanner “types” the ISBN and presses Enter. This works in the catalogue search, in Add book, in Quick capture and at the Circulation desk. At the desk a scanner is the fastest and most reliable option.

  • Nothing arrives? The cursor isn't in the field – click into it once.
  • No Enter afterwards? Set the scanner to “Enter/CR suffix” as described in its manual.
  • Wrong characters? The scanner's keyboard layout doesn't match the computer (e.g. US instead of German).

Camera (phone, tablet, laptop): click the camera icon or Scan with camera, allow camera access and hold the ISBN barcode inside the frame. Only a valid ISBN barcode (EAN-13 starting with 978/979) is accepted. Chrome, Edge and Android use their built-in detection, Firefox and Safari a bundled one – nothing is loaded from third-party servers.
