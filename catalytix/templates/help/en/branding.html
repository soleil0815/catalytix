Under Administration → Branding you adapt the library to your corporate design. The preview updates instantly; View sign-in page shows the result as visitors see it.

  • Logo (ideally PNG with transparent background, or SVG) – shown in the sidebar, on the sign-in page, in emails and as the browser icon. Options: Show the library name next to the logo and Show logo on a light background (for dark logos). SVGs with scripts or external references are rejected.
  • Colours: Main colour (buttons, links), Accent colour, Sidebar. Text colours are chosen automatically for readability, in dark mode too. Default resets a colour.
  • Sign-in page: Background image (landscape, at least 1600 px; it is downscaled and GPS data removed), dimming slider, Show bookshelf graphic, Small heading and Welcome text (empty = hidden).
  • Library name – can also be changed under Settings.

Apply with Save; Reset everything restores logo, image, colours and texts to the defaults. Emails pick up logo and colours automatically.

Note: the logo and background image can be fetched without signing in because the sign-in page needs them – don't use anything confidential. The files are kept in ./data/branding and survive updates.
