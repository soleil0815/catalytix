Groups link books and people, e.g. a class or a training programme. Every book belongs to one group or is general.

  • Borrowing: readers can only borrow books of their own groups and general books.
  • Visibility in the catalogue: {% if f.catalog_all %}currently everyone sees the whole stock{% else %}currently readers only see their groups' books and general books{% endif %} – change this under Settings → Catalogue visibility.
  • Librarians with a group only manage books and loans of their groups (plus general books). A librarian without a group is responsible for the whole stock.
  1. Administration → Groups → Group: enter name, colour and description.
  2. Tick users under Members on the right – or choose groups in the user's account.
  3. Save.

Automatically from Active Directory: enter the name (e.g. Class7a) or DN of a directory group under AD group (optional). Its members are added to this group at every sign-in and removed when they leave. This requires Assign library groups automatically to be switched on under Active Directory. Groups assigned manually without a directory link are left untouched.

Reference only (no lending): if you tick Reference only – books in this group are not lent out, the books stay visible in the catalogue (marked Reference only) but are no longer offered for lending anywhere: not at the circulation desk, not on the book page, not for self-checkout and not for reservations. Open reservations for these books are deleted when you save. Copies already on loan are returned as usual.

Deleting a group turns its books into general books – nothing is lost.
