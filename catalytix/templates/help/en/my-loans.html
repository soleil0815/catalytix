My loans shows everything you currently have, with loan date and due date. The Overdue and History tabs show only overdue loans or books you have already returned. The colour tells you the status: green = plenty of time, yellow = due soon, red = overdue.

{% if f.self_extend %}

It counts from the later of today and the current due date. Once the limit is reached the button disappears; after that only the library can renew.

Renewals can only be made by library staff here. Ask at the circulation desk in good time before the due date.

Returning: {% if f.self_return %}Return records the return yourself – then put the book back at its location.{% else %}Bring the book to the circulation desk; there is no return button for you.{% endif %}
