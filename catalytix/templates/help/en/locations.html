Under Management → Locations you define your shelves, rooms or cupboards once. As soon as at least one exists, the location is picked from a list when adding, quick-capturing and editing books instead of being typed – no more typos.

  • Add: write the names in the box, one per line, then click Add. Duplicates (also with different upper/lower case) are skipped.
  • Rename: change the name in its field and save with ✓ – all books at that location are updated too. An optional note helps people find it.
  • Delete only works once no book is located there.
  • The number next to each location leads to its books; the Catalogue can be filtered by location.

In the holdings, but not defined lists locations typed freely in the past. Choose add as new location or replace with … and click Apply – the books are updated too. For a different spelling (“regal a2”) or a similar name (“Regl A1”) the matching location is already preselected.

Merging: two defined locations cannot be merged by renaming. Move the books of one of them, then delete it.
