Administration → Users lists all local accounts and every Active Directory account that has signed in at least once. The drop-down filters by Awaiting approval, Self-registered, Disabled and Sign-in lock (failed attempts).

Roles: readers borrow books, librarians manage the stock and loans (only for their groups; without a group, the whole stock), administrators can do everything.

  1. Create: Local user – username, role, password (at least 10 characters), groups. Useful for guests or as an emergency admin account.
  2. Approve: confirm pending self-registrations in the list with Approve, or reject them with ✕ (the account is deleted).{% if f.pending_approvals %} {{ f.pending_approvals }} currently waiting.{% endif %}
  3. Disable rather than delete: untick Account active – the account can no longer sign in or borrow, but its history is kept. Delete removes the account and its loan history for good and only works when nothing is on loan. You cannot delete or disable yourself or the last administrator.

Fixing sign-in problems – no command line needed: open the account and scroll down to Sign-in and security:

  • Reset next to two-factor authentication removes the app and security keys; if 2FA is mandatory, the user has to set it up again. Any sign-in lock is lifted at the same time.
  • Lift lock releases an account immediately after too many failed attempts (otherwise it unlocks itself after 5 minutes, after very many failed attempts from different addresses after up to 15 minutes). Failed attempts from outside lock the account only for sign-ins from outside.

Setting a new password or resetting 2FA signs the account out of all other sessions – including “Stay signed in”.

Active Directory accounts: name, email and role are taken from the directory at every sign-in. Here you can only assign groups and disable the account; passwords are changed by IT in the directory.
