1. Click Add book (or + Book at the top).
  2. Enter the ISBN – by keyboard, with a barcode scanner (click into the field and scan) or via Camera.
  3. Fetch data (or Enter in the ISBN field) gets title, author, publisher, year, pages and cover from the German National Library, Open Library and Google Books. Give it a quick check.
  4. Under Holdings choose the group (General = anyone can borrow it), the number of copies, the shelf mark and the location.
  5. Save – or Save & next to add the next book straight away.
  • If the ISBN already exists, the app tells you – better to increase the copies there.
  • Only the title is required. Without an ISBN, fill everything in by hand.
  • Cover: enter an image URL or upload your own photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF). It is re-saved and metadata is removed.
  • Location: if locations are defined under Locations, you pick from a list; otherwise you type freely.

Editing and deleting: the book page has Edit and Delete. A book on loan cannot be deleted, and the number of copies cannot go below the number currently lent. Deleting also removes the book's loan history.
